# Art direction (A1)

GPT reference pictures from `docs/gpt_map_prompts.md`, saved in `art/source/gpt_map/` (style A,
"glossy blocks"). They set the mood and the props for the Blender kits (A3-A5); they are not used in
the game as images. Open points are listed at the end until the developer answers them.

Shared look: chunky smooth blocks with slightly rounded edges, saturated colours, bright green two-tone
checkerboard grass, warm sun from the upper left, soft shadows, sparkles and glow, blocky trees made of
stacked cubes, cute blocky cats with big round eyes peeking out of hiding places.
